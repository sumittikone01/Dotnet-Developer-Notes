# SELF JOIN

- The process of establishing connection with the same table is called self join.
- Joining a table by itself is called self join

| ANSI(Ameriacan national standard institute)                                          | ORACLE                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| SELECT COLUMN-NAME/EXPRESSION FROM TABLE-NAME1 JOIN TABLE_NAME2 ON <JOIN_CONDITION>; | SELECT COLUMN_NAME/EXPRESSION FROM TABLE TABLE_NAME1, TABLE_NAME2  WHERE <JOIN_CONDITION>; |
| TABLE1.COLUMN_NAME=TABLE2.COLUMN_NAME                                                | EMP.DEPTNO=DEPT.DEPTNO                                                                     |

WAQTD  EMP NAME  & THEIR ANUAL SAL ALONG WITH MANAGER’S NAME AND ANUAL SAL IF EMP ARE EARNING MORE THAN SMITH & MGR ARE WORKING IN THE LOCATION OF NEW YORK IF ALL OF THEM HAVE CHAR ‘A’ OR CHAR ‘S’ IN THEIR JOB

```sql
SELECT E.ENAME EMPNAME, M.ENAME MGRNAME, 
	E.SAL*12 EMPANUALSAL, M.SAL * 12 MGRANUALSAL 
FROM EMP E JOIN EMP M 
ON E.MGR=M.EMPNO 
WHERE E.SAL > (
		SELECT SAL 
		FROM EMP 
		WHERE ENAME = 'SMITH') 
AND M.DEPTNO= (
		SELECT DEPTNO 
		FROM DEPT 
		WHERE LOC='NEW YORK') 
AND (E.JOB LIKE '%A%' OR E.JOB LIKE '%S%') 
AND (M.JOB LIKE '%A%' OR M.JOB LIKE '%S%');
```

WAQTD EMP NAME MANAGERS NAME  AND MANAGERS MANAGER NAME IF EMP ARE EARNING MORE THAN 2000 AND MANAGERS ARE EARNING MORE THAN THEIR EMP AND MANAGER’S MANAGER ARE WORKING IN LOC OF CHIKAGO OR NEW YOURK

```sql
FROM EMP E JOIN EMP M

ON M.MGR=MM.EMPNO
```

WAQTD DETAILS OF EMP AND THEIR MANAGERS AND M’S MANAGER  IF  ALL OF THEM ARE NOT EARNING COMMITION AND EMP ARE EARNING MORE THAN THE EMP WHO ARE WORKING IN LOC OF NEW YORK AND MANAGERS ARE HIRE BEFORE THE EMP WHO DONT HAVE CHAR ‘A’ IN THEIR JOB AND MM NAMES ARE STARTING WITH THE OVELS

```sql
SELECT E.ENAME, M.ENAME AS MNAME, MM.ENAME AS MMNAME,E.EMPNO,M.EMPNO, MM.EMPNO
FROM EMP E JOIN EMP M
ON E.MGR=M.EMPNO JOIN EMP MM
ON M.MGR=MM.EMPNO
WHERE E.COMM IS NULL 
AND M.COMM IS NULL 
AND MM.COMM IS NULL 
AND E.SAL > ALL ( 
	SELECT SAL 
	FROM EMP 
	WHERE DEPTNO IN (
		SELECT DEPTNO 
		FROM DEPT 
		WHERE LOC='NEW YORK'
	)
) AND M.HIREDATE < ALL (
	SELECT HIREDATE
	FROM EMP 
	WHERE JOB NOT LIKE '%A%'
) AND SUBSTR(MM.ENAME,1,1) IN ('A','E','I','O','U');
```

WAQTD EMP DETAILS AND THIR MANAGERS DETAILS IF EMP ARE HIRED IN YEAR OF 1981 AND MANAGERS ARE HIRED ON WED AND MM’S HAVING 4 DIGIT SAL AND ALL OF THEM WORKING IN ACCOUNTING DEPT

```sql

FROM EMP E,EMP M,EMP MM, DEPT ED, DEPT MD, DEPT MMD
WHERE E.MGR=M.EMPNO AND 
M.MGR=MM.EMPNO AND 
E.DEPTNO=ED.DEPTNO AND 
M.DEPTNO=MD.DEPTNO AND 
MM.DEPTNO=MMD.DEPTNO AND 
E.HIREDATE >= '1-JAN-1981' AND 
E.HIREDATE < '1-JAN-1982' AND 
TO_CHAR(M.HIREDATE,'DY')='WED' AND 
MM.SAL LIKE '____' AND 
ED.DNAME='ACCOUNTING' AND 
MD.DNAME='ACCOUNTING' AND 
MMD.DNAME='ACCOUNTING' ;
```

WAQTD LOC OF SALESMAN’S MANAGERS MANAGER

```sql
SELECT E.ENAME, E.JOB ,MMD.LOC, MM.ENAME
FROM EMP E JOIN DEPT D 
ON E.DEPTNO=D.DEPTNO JOIN EMP M 
ON E.MGR=M.EMPNO JOIN DEPT MD
ON M.DEPTNO= MD.DEPTNO JOIN EMP MM
ON M.MGR=MM.EMPNO JOIN DEPT MMD
ON MM.DEPTNO=MMD.DEPTNO 
WHERE E.JOB='SALESMAN';
```
