1. Que-1

   <aside>
    ❓

   WAQTD NAME OF THE EMP AND HIS LOCATION OF ALL THE EMP

   ```sql
   SELECT ENAME,LOC
   FROM EMP INNER JOIN DEPT
   ON EMP.DEPTNO=DEPT.DEPTNO;
   ```

   ![image.png](ASSIGNMENT-JOINS(10)/image.png)

   </aside>
2. Que-2

   <aside>
    ❓

   WAQTD DNAME AND SAL FOR ALL EMP WORKING IN ACCOUNTING

   ```sql
   SELECT DNAME,SAL
   FROM EMP INNER JOIN DEPT
   ON EMP.DEPTNO=DEPT.DEPTNO
   WHERE DNAME='ACCOUNTING';
   ```

   ![image.png](<ASSIGNMENT-JOINS(10)/image%201.png>)

   </aside>
3. Que-3

   <aside>
    ❓

   WAQTD DNAME AND ANNUAL SALARY FOR ALL EMPLOYEES WHOSE SALARY IS MORE THAN 2340.

   ```sql
   SELECT DNAME, SAL*12 AS ANUAL_SALARY
   FROM EMP INNER JOIN DEPT
   ON EMP.DEPTNO=DEPT.DEPTNO
   WHERE SAL>2340;
   ```

   ![image.png](<ASSIGNMENT-JOINS(10)/image%202.png>)

   </aside>
4. Que-4

   <aside>
    ❓

   WAQTD ENAME AND DNAME FOR EMPLOYEES HAVING CHARACTER ‘A’ IN THEIR DNAME.

   ```sql
   SELECT ENAME, DNAME
   FROM EMP INNER JOIN DEPT
   ON EMP.DEPTNO=DEPT.DEPTNO
   WHERE DNAME LIKE '%A%';
   ```

   ![image.png](<ASSIGNMENT-JOINS(10)/image%203.png>)

   </aside>

6. Que-6

   <aside>
    ❓

   WAQTD DNAME AND JOB FOR ALL THE EMPLOYEES WHOSE JOB AND DNAME START WITH CHARACTER ‘S’.

   ```sql
   SELECT DNAME, JOB
   FROM EMP INNER JOIN DEPT
   ON EMP.DEPTNO=DEPT.DEPTNO
   WHERE JOB LIKE 'S%' AND DNAME LIKE 'S%';
   ```

8. Que-8

   <aside>
    ❓

   WAQTD DNAME AND HIREDATE FOR EMPLOYEES HIRED AFTER 83 IN ACCOUNTING OR RESEARCH DEPARTMENT.

   ```sql
   SELECT DNAME, HIREDATE
   FROM EMP INNER JOIN DEPT
   ON EMP.DEPTNO=DEPT.DEPTNO
   WHERE HIREDATE>= '1-JAN-1984' AND (JOB='ACCOUNTING' OR JOB='RESEARCH');
   ```

9. Que-9

   <aside>
    ❓

   WAQTD ENAME AND DNAME OF THE EMPLOYEES WHO ARE GETTING COMM IN DEPARTMENT 10 OR 30.

   ```sql
   SELECT ENAME, DNAME
   FROM EMP INNER JOIN DEPT
   ON EMP.DEPTNO=DEPT.DEPTNO
   WHERE COMM IS NOT NULL AND (EMP.DEPTNO=10 OR EMP.DEPTNO=30);
   ```

   ![image.png](<ASSIGNMENT-JOINS(10)/image%208.png>)

10. Que-10

    <aside>
    ❓

    WAQTD DNAME AND EMPNO FOR ALL THE EMPLOYEES WHOSE EMPNO ARE (7839, 7902) AND ARE WORKING IN LOCATION NEW YORK.

    ```sql
    SELECT ENAME, EMPNO
    FROM EMP INNER JOIN DEPT
    ON EMP.DEPTNO=DEPT.DEPTNO
    WHERE LOC='NEW YORK' AND (EMPNO=7839 OR EMPNO=7902);
    ```
