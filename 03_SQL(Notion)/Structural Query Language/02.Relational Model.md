# Relational Model

## **Relational Model**

1. The **Relational Model** was designed by the famous computer scientist **Edgar Frank Codd (E. F. Codd)** in **1970**.
2. In this model, **data is stored in the form of rows and columns**.
3. The combination of rows and columns forms a **table (relation)**.
4. Any **DBMS** that follows the **Relational Model** is known as a **Relational Database Management System (RDBMS)**.
5. A DBMS that adheres to **E. F. Codd’s Rules** qualifies as a **true RDBMS**.
