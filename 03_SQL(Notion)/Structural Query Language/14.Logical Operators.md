# Logical Operators

Logical operators are used to **combine multiple conditions**

# 1) AND (Binary Multiplication operator)

- Returns **TRUE only if all conditions are TRUE**.
- It is used whenever we want to satisfy multiple conditions together.
- Syntax:

  ```sql
  SELECT COLUMN_NAME/EXP
  FROM TABLE_NAME
  WHERE CONDITION1 AND CONDITION2 AND CONDITION3......;
  ```
- Examples:

  ```sql
  SELECT *
  FROM EMP
  WHERE SAL > 2000 AND JOB= 'ANALYST';
  ```

# 2) OR (BINARY ADDITION OPERATOR)

- Returns **TRUE if *any one* condition is TRUE**.
- It is used whenever we want to satisfy anyone of the condition.
- Syntax:

  ```sql
  SELECT COLUMN_NAME/EXP
  FROM TABLE_NAME
  WHERE CONDITION1 OR CONDITION2 OR CONDITION3......;
  ```
- Examples:

  ```sql
  SELECT * 
  FROM EMP
  WHERE SAL > 2000 OR JOB = 'KING';
  ```

  <aside>
    ❓

  WAQTD NAME DEPTNO, HALFTERM SALARY OF THE EMPLOYEES ALONG WITH DETAILS IF EMPLOYEES ARE WORKING DEPTNO 10, 20, 30, 40

  ```sql
  SELECT EMP.*, ENAME, DEPTNO, SAL*6
  FROM EMP
  WHERE DEPTNO = 10 OR DEPTNO = 20 OR DEPTNO = 30 OR DEPTNO = 40;
  ```

  </aside>

  <aside>
    ❓

  WAQTD JOB, HIREDATE OF THE EMPLOYEES WHO ARE EARNING MORE THAN 2000 AND WORKING AS ANALYST OR CLERK

  ```sql
  SELECT JOB, HIREDATE
  FROM EMP
  WHERE SAL>2000 AND (JOB = 'ANALYST' OR JOB = 'CLERK');
  ```

  </aside>

  <aside>

  WAQTD DETAILS OF EMP ALONG WITH 40% DECREMENT IN THE SALARY IF THEY ARE WORKING AS ‘ANALYST’ OR ‘CLERK’ OR ‘SALSEMAN’ AND THEY ARE HIRED IN THE YEAR OF 1981 AND THEY ARE WORKING IN DEPTNO 10 OR 30 IF THEIR ANNUAL SALARY IS MORE THAN 8000 AND HALF TERM SALARY IS < 50000


  </aside>

# 3. NOT

- It is used whenever we want to inverse or reject the condition.
- Syntax:

  ```sql
  SELECT COLUMN_NAME/EXP
  FROM TABLE_NAME
  WHERE NOT CONDITION
  ```
- Examples:

  <aside>
    ❓

  WAQTD Details of Employee if they are not working as clerk

  ```sql
    SELECT * 
    FROM EMP
    WHERE NOT JOB = 'CLERK';
  ```

  OR

  ```sql
  SELECT *
  FROM EMP
  WHERE JOB != 'CLERK';
  ```

  </aside>

  <aside>
    ❓

  WAQTD Details of emp if they are not working as clerk or analyst

  ```sql
  SELECT *
  FROM EMP
  WHERE NOT (JOB = 'CLERK' OR JOB = 'ANALYST');
  ```

  </aside>
